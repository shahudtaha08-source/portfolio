Who's behind the system

Taha Shahud is a Computer Science Engineering student in his third year at MGM University's School of Engineering and Technology, specializing in Data Science.

Outside of coursework — labs, theory assessments, and exams — he spends his time building: client software, personal AI companions, automation tools, and experimental products. Some ship, some stall, some are still in progress. All of them are real attempts at learning by building rather than just following tutorials.

His projects often carry a personal thread — several combine Islamic cultural elements, like prayer reminders and Hinglish/Urdu language support, with the technical systems he's exploring: on-device AI, natural language interfaces, and automation.
